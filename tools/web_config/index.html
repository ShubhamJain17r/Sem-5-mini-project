Sub-Millimeter Tabletop Picking Repeatability Guidelines
  • Resolution: 1 servo tick ≈ 0.24°. At reach R = 250 mm, 0.24° translates to ~1.05 mm fingertip displacement.
  • Repeatability Threshold: To guarantee sub-millimeter picking (<1.0 mm), tracking errors must be maintained within ≤ 1 tick error.
  • Maintenance Rule 1: If tracking standard deviation σ > 3 ticks over 20 cycles, retorque servo horn M3 lock screws.
  • Maintenance Rule 2: Recalibrate software deviation trims if mean offset μ drifts by > ±5 ticks.
