📌 Backup Notes & Camera Homography Tips
  1. Why Backup Markers? In physical robotics setups, tape peel, adhesive slippage, or surface scuffs can degrade sub-pixel corner refinement. Keep these 4 spare markers handy.
  2. Sub-Pixel Refinement: The ROS 2 vision pipeline uses CORNER_REFINE_SUBPIX with cv2.aruco.DICT_4X4_50. Clean, sharp cuts with flat adhesion ensure sub-2 mm centroid error.
  3. Lighting Consideration: Ensure uniform, non-glare overhead illumination. If using glossy sticker paper, avoid direct specular reflections from spotlights into the overhead camera lens.
